The month of the second round and the season's warnings closed at the end of August, and the closing brought the autumn's arguments into view on the calendar that the year's second half has fixed. The trade follow-ups that the Beijing round scheduled, the budget season that the governments will conduct, the summits and the sessions that the institutions have set, all of it is now dated and scheduled, and the scheduling is the preparation that the summer has enabled; August's record was the record of the preparation, and the preparation is the part that the autumn will test.

The trade story of the month was the story of the largest file's movement. The Beijing round, concluded in the month's second week, moved the negotiations between the world's two largest economies further than the spring's rounds had, with the framework sketched and the follow-ups scheduled; the movement has changed the expectations of the markets and the governments, and the follow-ups will conduct the work that the framework has opened. The month's trade record is the record of the movement, and the movement's outcome will be measured by the autumn's sessions.

The summer's other stories continued through the month with the weight that the year has accumulated. The ceasefire that the summer opened held through its weeks, with the monitors' reports and the negotiators' progress providing the war's quiet news; the alliance's adjustment to the drawdown proceeded through the plans and the budgets that the European capitals are preparing; and the season's warnings, the storms and the heat, kept the climate in the conversation that the autumn's summits will carry. The month's record is the record of the continuations, and the continuations will be the autumn's subjects.

The autumn's arguments, as August closes, are the ones that the calendar has lined up. The trade follow-ups will test the framework that the Beijing round sketched, and the test will be conducted in the sessions that the negotiators have scheduled; the budget season will test the rearmament and the welfare state against each other in the parliaments of the member states; and the summits, the climate gathering and the security meetings, will test the year's adjustments against the institutions' agendas. The arguments are the year's second half, and the second half's shape is now visible on the calendar.

The preparation that the summer enabled is the part that the autumn will now consume, and the consumption will be conducted at the pace that the year has established. The settlements that the summer produced will be implemented, the frameworks that the talks sketched will be tested, and the budgets and the summits will be conducted against the deadlines that the calendars have fixed; the autumn's arguments will be the year's climax, and the climax's outcome will be determined by the preparations that the summer has completed. August closed with the autumn's arguments lining up, and the lining is the calendar's promise and its burden: the year's second half will be the season of the decisions, and the decisions will be made in the sessions and the summits that the month of preparation has set in motion.
