The year's final days brought the reading that the turning requires, and the reading assembled the accounts of 2025 with the particular completeness that the year's end provides. The conflicts and the settlements, the decisions and the deferrals, the records and the rituals that the year has produced, all of it was entered into the ledger that the turning will close; the reading is the year's conclusion, and the conclusion's form will be the new year's beginning. The accounts were weighed with the particular care that the year's events command, and the weighing is the turning's work.

The year's conflicts were the ledger's largest entries, and the entries' course was the one that the seasons have documented. The tariff era opened with the escalation and settled into the negotiations that produced the agreements; the war's ceasefire held through its months with the settlement's terms still being negotiated; and the institutions that the year tested, the alliances and the mechanisms, were renegotiated through the seasons' events; the conflicts are the year's record, and the record's weighing is the ledger's central work.

The year's decisions were the entries that the institutions delivered: the elections and the transitions, the verdicts and the summits, the climate finance and the budgets that the governments concluded. The decisions were delivered through the calendars that the year set, and the delivery is the record of the institutions' work; the decisions' weighing, conducted in the final reading, is the measurement of the year's institutional record.

The year's economic entries were the ones that the ledger's middle columns carry, and the entries' course was the one that the markets and the households experienced. The prices that the tariffs raised and the adjustments that the households made, the rates that the central banks set and the trade that the agreements restored, all of it was entered into the account that the year's commerce produced; the economic entries are the ledger's daily columns, and the daily columns are the ones that the year's record is built from. The economy was the year's running total, and the total's final figure is the one that the new year will begin from.

The year's quieter entries are the ones that the ledger's final columns carry: the science that was published, the rituals that were kept, the seasons that were observed, and the households that held their courses through the year's events. The quieter entries are the year's counterpoint, and the counterpoint's weighing is the ledger's completeness; the year's record is not only the conflicts and the decisions but the continuity that the institutions and the households provided.

The new year's beginning, as the ledger's reading closes, will be the beginning that the year's accounts have set up: the negotiations that the follow-ups will continue, the implementations that the settlements require, and the calendars that the institutions have fixed. The beginning will be conducted with the record that the year has produced, and the conduct will be the ledger's next chapter; the accounts of 2025 have been assembled, the entries have been weighed, and the reading is the conclusion that the turning requires. The year's ledger was closed with the particular care that the year's events command, and the closing is the preparation for the new year that the turning will open, which is the way that the years have always kept their accounts.
