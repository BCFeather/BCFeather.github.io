The climate summit closed in Belém at the end of the third week of November, and the closing delivered the verdict that the two weeks of negotiations had produced. The finance decision was concluded, the texts were delivered, and the gathering's record was written in the presence of the year's pressures; the closing ceremonies were conducted with the particular combination of the relief and the weighing that the summit's ends always carry. The verdict is the year's climate record in its final form, and the record's meaning will be weighed by the sessions and the summits that follow.

The finance decision is the part of the verdict that the gathering's attention will keep returning to. The numbers were concluded through the final sessions, the commitments were fixed and the mechanisms were established, and the decision's scale will be measured against the year's documented needs; the vulnerable countries' delegates received the decision with the mixture of the acceptance and the insistence that the gap's persistence warrants, and the developed countries' negotiators presented it with the language of the progress that the gathering could deliver. The finance is the summit's central product, and the product's adequacy will be the gathering's lasting question.

The texts that the summit delivered are the quieter part of the verdict, and the quiet is the measure of the work's completion. The documents that the gathering concluded, the mitigation language, the adaptation plans, the mechanisms that the year's negotiations had assembled, all of it was delivered in the forms that the sessions had settled; the texts are the gathering's foundation, and the foundation's strength will be tested by the implementation that the coming years will conduct. The products are the summit's record, and the record's completeness is the gathering's contribution.

The gathering's place in the year is the part of the verdict that the commentators have begun to weigh. The summit conducted its work in the year of the largest economy's absence, and the conduct demonstrated the institution's capacity to proceed through the departure; the finance decision was concluded at the scale that the politics allowed, and the scale will be judged against the year's records and the years' needs; the gathering's verdict is the record of what the year's climate politics could produce, and the record's sufficiency is the question that the coming years will answer. The summit is the year's chapter, and the chapter's meaning will be read through the sessions that follow.

The summit's closing returned the delegates to their capitals with the texts and the decisions that the gathering produced, and the returning is the work's resumption; the finance will be implemented, the texts will be processed, and the negotiations will continue through the sessions that the institution schedules. The climate summit closed in the Amazonian city with the verdict that the two weeks produced, and the verdict is the year's climate record in its final form; the finance was concluded, the texts were delivered, and the gathering's meaning will be weighed by the years that the decision will be tested in, which is the way that the climate process has always worked.
