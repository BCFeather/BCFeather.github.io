The week before the climate summit's opening brought the final preparations into view, and the view carried the particular combination of hope and tension that the gathering's approach always produces. The delegations were assembling, the negotiators arriving in the Amazonian city that the summit has made the season's centre; the texts were in their near-final drafts, the products of the sessions that the year's negotiations have conducted; and the finance question, the summit's central test, remained unresolved, the gap that the gathering will have to confront. The eve of Belém was the summit's last quiet moment, and the quiet was the preparation's final phase.

The texts that the negotiators have assembled are the part of the preparation that will be tested first. The agreements that the year's sessions have produced, the mitigation language, the adaptation plans, the mechanisms that the previous summits established, all of it has been drafted into the documents that the summit will negotiate from, and the drafts' completeness is measured by the finance gap that they leave; the texts are the summit's foundation, and the foundation's strength will be tested by the negotiations that the gathering will conduct. The preparation is the year's work in its final form, and the form's fate will be decided in the city.

The finance question is the one that the summit's outcome will hinge on, and the question's state, on the eve, was the state that the year has produced: the commitments made and unfunded, the largest economy's departure reducing the contributors, and the vulnerable countries' insistence making the funding the gathering's central test; the gap is the arithmetic that the negotiators will confront, and the confrontation will be the summit's most difficult work. The finance is the summit's question, and the question's answer will be the gathering's verdict.

The delegations' arrivals carried the politics that the year has established. The host country's government has prepared the staging that the gathering's importance warrants, the coalitions of the vulnerable countries have organized with the particular energy that the emergencies give them, and the larger economies have calibrated their positions to the domestic pressures that the year has produced; the arrivals are the politics' assembly, and the assembly will shape the summit's sessions. The gathering's politics will be conducted in the presence of the year's records, and the records are the summit's backdrop.

The summit's opening, scheduled for the coming days, will begin the negotiation that the year has been preparing for, and the negotiation will be conducted through the weeks that the gathering schedules; the finance question will be confronted, the texts will be completed, and the institution's capacity will be demonstrated or questioned in the year of the largest economy's absence. The eve of Belém carried the hope and the tension that the approach always produces, and the combination is the summit's context; the delegations are assembling, the texts are drafted, and the gathering that the year has been building toward is about to begin.
