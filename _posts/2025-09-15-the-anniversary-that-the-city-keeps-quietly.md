The mid-September anniversary arrived with the particular quiet that the city reserves for it, and the quiet was the day's most faithful register. The names were read, as they are read every year, at the ceremony that the families attend; the moments of silence were observed at the times that the day fixes; and the memorials received the visitors, the families, the officials, the school groups, who make the pilgrimage that the day commands; the rituals were conducted with the regularity that twenty-four years have established. The day is kept by the city and the country with the ceremonies that the grief has built, and the keeping is the part of the anniversary that the coverage cannot improve.

The names that were read at the ceremony are the day's permanent subject, and the reading is the ritual that the families insist on. The lists, the fire, the towers, the field, the names that the country has learned to hear, were read through the morning with the particular care that the occasion demands; the reading is the guarantee that the names are not reduced to the numbers, and the guarantee is the ceremony's purpose. The families who attended the ceremony carried the grief that the years have made more bearable and no less present, and the presence is the day's quiet content.

The rituals that the day has accumulated are the ones that the city performs with the regularity of the calendar. The flag ceremonies, the moments of silence, the evening services, all of it was conducted through the day with the precision that the memorial culture has developed; the rituals are the form that the grief has found, and the form's persistence is the demonstration of the day's place in the city's life. The rituals continue, and the continuing is the answer that the city gives to the questions that the day raises.

The day's politics were the quieter part of the anniversary this year. The officials attended the ceremonies with the statements that the occasion requires, and the statements were delivered with the particular restraint that the year's security debates have made appropriate; the country's remembrance was conducted alongside its arguments about the wars and the threats that have followed, and the alongside is the day's contemporary context. The anniversary was kept with the politics at the margins, and the margins were the day's appropriate place for them.

The anniversary's meaning, twenty-four years on, is the meaning that the keeping itself provides. The day is kept because the loss is kept, and the loss is kept because the day's events changed the country's course in ways that the years have made familiar; the remembrance is the country's way of holding the date against the ordinary passage of the years, and the holding is the ritual's value. The mid-September anniversary arrived with the particular quiet that the city reserves for it, and the quiet was observed through the reading of the names, the moments of silence and the visits to the memorials; the day was kept, as it is kept every year, by the families, the officials and the city, and the keeping is the part of the anniversary that the years cannot diminish and the coverage cannot improve.
