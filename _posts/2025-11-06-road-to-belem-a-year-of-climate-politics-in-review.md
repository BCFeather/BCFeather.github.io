The summit that opens in the Amazonian city this month is the product of a year of climate politics, and the year's record is the one that the gathering will negotiate from. The largest economy's withdrawal from the agreement, completed in the spring, has shaped every session that the year has conducted; the finance gap, the commitments made and unfunded, has been the negotiations' central subject; the records of heat that the year has set have provided the urgency that the sessions have operated under; and the negotiations themselves, conducted through the months of the year, have produced the texts that the summit will now test. The road to Belém was built by the year's events, and the road's condition is the summit's starting point.

The year's climate politics opened with the withdrawal, and the withdrawal's consequences have run through the year. The largest economy's departure removed the contributor and the convenor, the other powers have competed for the leadership that the departure opened, and the competition has shaped the positions that the summit will confront; the departure is the year's climate event, and the event's consequences are the summit's context. The politics that the year has produced are the politics of the transition, and the transition is the road's first stretch.

The finance question has been the road's central construction, and the construction's state is the one that the summit will confront. The commitments that the developed countries made have not been funded at the levels promised, the gaps have been documented through the year's sessions, and the vulnerable countries have made the funding the gathering's central test; the finance is the road's unfinished section, and the finishing will be the summit's most difficult work. The question's answer will be the gathering's verdict, and the verdict's shape will be determined by the negotiations that the summit will conduct.

The records that the year has set have provided the road's urgency, and the urgency is the pressure that the negotiations operate under. The heat records, the fires, the storms, the floods, all of it has been documented through the year's coverage, and the documentation has fed the negotiations' language and the publics' attention; the records are the summit's backdrop, and the backdrop's weight is the gathering's moral arithmetic. The urgency is real, and the urgency's translation into the finance will be the summit's test.

The summit that the road has led to will be conducted through the weeks that the gathering schedules, and the conduct will be the year's climate record in its final form; the finance will be confronted, the texts will be completed, and the institution's capacity will be tested in the year of the largest economy's absence. The road to Belém was built by the year's events, and the road's condition, the departure's consequences, the finance gap, the records' urgency, is the summit's starting point; the gathering that the year has been preparing for is about to begin, and the beginning will be the test that the road has been leading to.
