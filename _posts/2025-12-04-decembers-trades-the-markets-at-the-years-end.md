The markets moved into December with the trades that the year's end has always produced, and the trades' shape was the one that the year's events have established. The positioning for the year's close began with the particular care that the institutional calendars command, the attention to the year-end data was fixed on the releases that the month's early weeks will deliver, and the weighing of the year's record, the tariff era's arithmetic, the monetary path, the geopolitical events, was conducted against the expectations that the new year will carry; December's markets are the year's final trading, and the trading's outcome will be the year's financial record.

The year's record, as the month's trading began, is the record that the seasons have assembled. The markets absorbed the tariff era's shocks and settlements, the inflation that the tariffs fed and the cooling that the conflict imposed, and the central bank's path that the data has set; the record is the year's financial story, and the story's final chapter is the one that the month's trading will write. The record's reading, conducted by the analysts and the strategists, is the preparation for the year's close.

The year-end positioning is the part of December's trading that the institutional calendars have fixed. The portfolios are being adjusted for the year's close, the gains and the losses weighed against the benchmarks that the funds track, and the positions are being set for the new year with the particular care that the turnover's costs require; the positioning is the markets' annual housekeeping, and the housekeeping is the month's machinery. The positioning's effects will be visible in the volume and the ranges that the month's sessions produce.

The year-end data is the part of December's trading that the central banks and the analysts will weigh. The inflation and the employment readings that the month will deliver will be the year's final data points, and the readings will feed the expectations that the new year's path will be set from; the data is the markets' guide, and the guide's final instalment is the month's particular contribution. The data's course will shape the positioning that the month's end will complete.

The year's sectors, weighed in the month's trading, are the ones that the year's record has written: the domestic industries that the tariff era favoured, the international supply chains that the conflicts tested, the technology and the energy that the year's growth carried, and the defence industries that the rearmament has filled. The sectors' year-end readings, conducted by the strategists, are the map that the new year's rotation will follow; the map is the year's market geography, and the geography's drawing is the month's analytical work.

The new year's expectations, as the month's trading begins, are the expectations that the year's record has produced: the trade negotiations' continuation, the monetary path's data-dependence, and the geopolitical calendar's uncertainties. The expectations will be priced through the month's sessions, and the pricing will be the year's final trading; December's markets moved into the month with the trades that the year's end has always produced, and the trading's outcome will be the year's financial record in its final form; the positioning is under way, the data is scheduled, and the year's financial story is being completed in the sessions that the month holds.
