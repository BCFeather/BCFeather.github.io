The middle of October brought the light that the season reserves for itself, the particular slant of the autumn sun that the northern latitudes have always known, and the pace that the light seems to set: the shorter days, the slower afternoons, the year's business conducted in the golden intervals between the mornings and the evenings. The light was the month's quiet gift, and the gift was the counterpoint to the year's headlines, the negotiations and the summits and the deadlines that the coverage carries; the counterpoint is worth the noticing, because the season's own pace is the one that the year's events cannot set.

The light's quality is the part that the painters and the poets have been documenting for as long as the seasons have been observed. The low angle, the long shadows, the particular warmth of the colour that the low sun casts, all of it is the autumn's signature, and the signature's appearance through the month is the demonstration that the natural calendar still runs beneath the political one; the demonstration is the season's reassurance, and the reassurance is the part that the coverage cannot supply. The light returned with the regularity that the sun's own calendar provides, and the regularity is the season's content.

The pace that the light sets is the part of the month that the towns and the cities have always absorbed. The afternoons that the earlier sun shortens, the evenings that the darkness claims earlier, the routines that the season adjusts, all of it is the autumn's annual adjustment, and the adjustment is the demonstration of the human calendars bending to the natural one; the bending is the season's work, and the work is conducted with the particular acceptance that the autumn always receives. The pace is the season's own, and the owning is the counterpoint's meaning.

The year's events, read against the season's light, are the record that the autumn's golden intervals frame: the negotiations, the decisions, the wars and the summits that the coverage carries, all of it conducted in the rooms that the light reaches and the days that the season shortens. The framing is the reminder that the year's business is conducted within the natural calendar, the calendar that the autumn is now closing; the reminder is the season's perspective, and the perspective is the value that the noticing provides. The year's events will continue, and the light will continue to slant, and the two continuings are the autumn's double rhythm.

The season's pace will carry through the weeks that the calendar holds, the golden intervals will shorten with the month's course, and the year's business will be conducted within them with the particular attention that the season's light seems to command; the autumn is the year's closing, and the closing is conducted in the light that the season reserves. The October light arrived with the slant that the northern latitudes know, and the pace that it set was the season's own; the counterpoint was kept, the afternoons were golden, and the year's business, framed by the light, moved through the intervals that the season provides, which is the way that the autumn always works.
