The month that carried the truce, the conclave and the Korean campaign's climax closed with the summer's calendar taking shape, and the shape was the one that the year's rhythm had been building since January. The trade negotiations' deadlines, the election in Seoul, the summits and the sessions that the season will hold, all of it is now scheduled, dated and circulated; the summer will be the season of the consequences, the season when the year's openings either close or widen. The month's events, read together, were the demonstration of the year's rhythm, and the rhythm is the thing that the summer will now have to keep.

The month's trade story moved through its most consequential turn, and the turn's meaning is still being weighed. The truce that the administration announced in the month's third week, the thirty-day window that both sides have accepted, reset the conflict that the spring had escalated, and the window's progress will be the summer's first trade story; the negotiations that the window opens will be conducted through the sessions that the calendars have scheduled, and the outcome will be the year's largest economic variable. The month's truce was the first real opening that the conflict has produced, and the opening's fate will be decided by the work of the weeks ahead.

The month's other story was the conclave, and the conclave's meaning will be longer in the unfolding. The election of the first pope from Asia, conducted on the first ballots, set the church on the path that the late pope's appointments had prepared, and the path's direction, the pastoral priorities, the dialogue, the growing churches, will be written by the new pontificate's years; the month's election was the rare event that the world's attention gathered around, and the attention will follow the new pope's first months with the particular interest that the church's choices still command. The conclave closed one chapter and opened another, and the opening is the story that the summer will begin to tell.

The Korean campaign's climax, scheduled for the first days of June, is the month's closing event, and the event's outcome will be the year's largest political story in the region. The campaign's final week was conducted in the shadow of the martial-law night, and the vote will deliver the country's answer to the winter's question; the answer will shape the country's politics through the term, and the shaping will be followed by the neighbours and the allies with the attention that the region's stability commands. The June election will close the spring's Korean chapter, and the closing will be the beginning of the country's next argument.

The summer's calendar, as May closes, is the calendar that the year's rhythm has produced: the trade deadlines, the elections, the summits, the sessions that the institutions have scheduled. The calendar's events will be the year's consequences, the tests of the openings that the spring created and the continuations of the conflicts that the spring could not settle; the summer will be conducted at the pace that the year has established, and the pace is the thing that the month's events have trained the world to expect. May closed with the calendar taking shape, and the shape is the schedule that the summer will keep, or that the summer's events will rewrite, which is the way of the calendars that the years of change produce.
