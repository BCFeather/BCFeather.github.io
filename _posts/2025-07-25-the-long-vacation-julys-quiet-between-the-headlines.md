The middle of July brought the particular quiet that the northern summer reserves for itself, and the quiet was the part of the season that the coverage has no headline for. The parliaments began their recesses, the news cycles slowed to the rhythms that the summer always imposes, and the world's attention drifted, with the particular gravity that the season exerts, toward the beaches and the mountains that the heat has always claimed; the quiet was the year's counterpoint, and the counterpoint was worth a moment's notice before the autumn's events arrive. The summer's pause is the calendar's oldest habit, and the habit's persistence is the reassurance that the year's pace has made more valuable.

The political calendar observed the pause in its own way. The leaders' schedules thinned, the summits and the sessions were spaced to accommodate the recesses, and the statements that the summer produced carried the measured tone that the season imposes; the work continued, as the work always does, through the channels and the officials that the recesses leave in place, and the continuation was the demonstration of the institutions' capacity to run on the reduced schedules. The summer's politics are the politics of the preparation, and the preparation is the quiet work that the autumn's events will test.

The markets observed the pause with the particular character that the summer always brings. The volumes declined with the traders' holidays, the ranges narrowed with the absence of the catalysts, and the analysts' notes were written with the lightness that the season permits; the summer's markets are the markets at the reduced speed, and the reduction is the annual demonstration of the market's dependence on the human calendars. The quiet markets were the backdrop of the summer's news, and the backdrop will be replaced, in the autumn, by the volume that the events will bring.

The summer's culture carried the season's particular pleasures, and the pleasures were the ones that the year's intensity had made more welcome. The festivals that the European cities stage, the readings and the concerts that the summer programs, the travel that the holidays release, all of it was performed with the particular appreciation that the crowded year has taught; the season's culture is the counterweight to the year's news, and the counterweight is the part that the coverage of the events rarely reaches. The summer's pleasures were taken, and the taking was the season's quiet content.

The quiet will end, as the summer's quiets always end, with the calendars that the autumn will bring: the sessions resuming, the deadlines approaching, the events that the year's second half has scheduled. The preparation that the summer has enabled will be tested, and the testing will be conducted at the pace that the year has established; the pause was the season's gift, and the gift's value will be measured by the autumn's work. The long vacation of July was the year's counterpoint, the quiet between the headlines, and the quiet was the season's own; the beaches and the mountains claimed their share of the world's attention, the parliaments and the markets ran at the reduced speed, and the year's business, held in the pause, resumed its course with the autumn that the calendar has scheduled.
