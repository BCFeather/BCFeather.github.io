The largest file is moving, and the movement's pace will be the month's trade story.

The summer's products are being consumed, and the consumption is the autumn's trade foundation.

The deadlines that the pause calendar has fixed are the part of the month that gives the negotiations their urgency. The trade clock is turning, and the turning is the mechanism by which the year's conflicts move.
