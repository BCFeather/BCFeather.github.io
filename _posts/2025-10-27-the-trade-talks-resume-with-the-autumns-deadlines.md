The trade negotiations resumed in the last week of October with the deadlines that the autumn has fixed and the follow-ups that the summer's frameworks scheduled, and the resumption carried the particular weight that the year's final quarter imposes. The sessions that the negotiators have scheduled will test whether the year's diplomacy can convert the frameworks that the summer sketched into the settlements that the calendar requires; the largest file's follow-ups, the European implementation and the other partners' talks, all of it is now on the table that the autumn has set. The resumption is the year's trade story in its decisive phase, and the decisiveness is the measure of the stakes.

The largest file's state, as the talks resumed, is the state that the summer's progress produced. The framework that the Beijing round sketched has been carried into the technical work that the autumn has conducted, the sectors and the timelines mapped against the positions that the two governments hold, and the political terms, the ones that the framework could not settle, are the subject of the sessions that the week will hold; the state is the progress's product, and the product's completion will be tested by the sessions' course. The talks are the year's largest negotiation, and the negotiation's outcome will be the year's largest trade variable.

The deadlines that the autumn has fixed are the part of the resumption that concentrates the negotiators' work. The dates that the pauses and the programmes have established are approaching on the calendar, and the approaches have accelerated the sessions that the negotiators have scheduled; the deadlines are the leverage that both sides hold, and the leverage's application is visible in the intensity of the preparations. The calendar is the negotiation's clock, and the clock's ticking is the resumption's environment.

The implementation of the summer's settlements is the quieter part of the autumn's trade story, and the quiet is the measure of the work's solidity. The British and the European deals are being processed through the mechanisms that their texts establish, the tariff reductions are being applied on the schedules that the agreements fixed, and the trade flows are being measured against the forecasts that the economists prepared; the implementation is the diplomacy's conversion into the commerce, and the conversion is the settlements' real test. The summer's products are being consumed, and the consumption is the autumn's trade foundation.

The resumption's outcome will be determined by the sessions' course and the politics that the two governments bring to them, and the determining will be conducted through the week and the follow-ups that the negotiators will schedule; the settlements that the resumption could produce would complete the year's trade story, and the deadlocks that it could produce would carry the conflict into the new year. The trade talks resumed with the autumn's deadlines and the summer's frameworks, and the resumption is the year's trade diplomacy at its most consequential; the sessions are scheduled, the deadlines are fixed, and the year's largest negotiation is now being conducted in the meetings that the autumn holds.
