The central bankers' annual gathering in the Wyoming mountains opened in the third week of August, and the gathering opened with the question that the year has made central: the path of the rates through the tariff-era inflation and the cooling growth, and the independence that the gathering's rituals are designed to affirm. The economists, the market strategists and the journalists assembled for the sessions that the gathering schedules, and the assembled attention was fixed on the speeches that the officials would deliver; the speeches at the gathering will set the expectations for the autumn's decisions, and the expectations are the gathering's product.

The economic context of the gathering is the one that the year's events have produced. The inflation measures have carried the tariff-driven price increases, the growth data has shown the cooling that the trade conflict has imposed, and the labour market has held the resilience that the central bank's framework weighs; the combination is the dilemma that the officials have been describing through the year, and the dilemma's management will be the subject of the gathering's sessions. The data that the officials carry to Wyoming is the data of the trade-off, and the trade-off is the central bank's current problem.

The expectations that the markets have priced are the part of the gathering's context that the speeches will have to address. The futures have carried the pricing of the rate path that the year's data has implied, and the pricing has moved with the inflation and the growth readings through the summer; the central bank's own projections, published through the year's meetings, have shown the path that the institution sees, and the gap between the market's pricing and the institution's projections is the space where the gathering's speeches will land. The expectations are the market's reading of the institution, and the reading is the gathering's live subject.

The independence question is the part of the gathering that the year has made unavoidable. The administration's public statements about the rates have continued through the summer, and the statements have been the backdrop of the institution's communications; the gathering's rituals, the podium, the speeches, the protocol, are the institution's performance of its independence, and the performance is the part that the officials will conduct with the particular care that the year requires. The independence is the institution's asset, and the asset's maintenance is the gathering's quiet work.

The autumn's decisions will be prepared by the gathering's speeches and the data that will follow them, and the preparation will be conducted through the sessions that the calendar has scheduled. The rate path will be set by the inflation and the growth that the autumn's data delivers, the framework that the institution maintains, and the independence that the year has tested; the decisions will be delivered through the meetings that the calendar has fixed, and the decisions will be the central bank's answer to the year's trade-off. Jackson Hole arrived with the rates in question, and the question's answer will be prepared in the Wyoming sessions and delivered through the autumn's meetings; the gathering has opened, the speeches will be made, and the expectations that the year's monetary policy depends on will be set by the words that the officials choose.
