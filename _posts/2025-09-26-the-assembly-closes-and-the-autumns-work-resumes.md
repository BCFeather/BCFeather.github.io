The general assembly's autumn session closed at the end of September, and the closing returned the world's diplomacy to the channels that the session had convened for. The record that the session produced, the speeches, the bilaterals, the conversations in the corridors, was the measure of the business that the year has accumulated; the leaders returned to their capitals with the commitments and the agendas that the session's margins had built, and the negotiators returned to the files that the autumn's calendar holds. The assembly's value was the room it provided, and the room's closing is the resumption of the work that the room made possible.

The session's diplomatic record is the part that the analysts have been weighing since the closing. The bilaterals that the session scheduled, the leaders' meetings that the margins produced, carried the year's arguments into the conversations that the agendas had prepared; the war's negotiations moved through the margins, the trade files were advanced through the bilaterals, and the climate finance was pressed through the sessions with the particular urgency that the autumn's summit has fixed; the record is the accumulation, and the accumulation is the diplomacy's progress, such as it is. The session's value was the concentration, and the concentration's products are the files that the autumn will now carry.

The war's diplomacy, as the session closed, is at the point that the summer and the autumn have produced. The ceasefire that opened in July has held through its weeks, the monitors have filed their reports, and the negotiators have continued the work that the settlement requires; the assembly's margins provided the moments for the conversations that the channels cannot carry, and the moments' products will be measured by the war's course through the autumn. The settlement remains distant, and the distance is the work that the negotiators will now resume in the channels that the assembly's closing has returned them to.

The autumn's calendar, as the assembly closed, is the calendar that the year's second half has fixed: the climate summit that the finance question will test, the trade follow-ups that the frameworks require, the security meetings that the year's adjustments demand. The calendar's events will be conducted with the preparations that the summer and the assembly have enabled, and the conduct will be measured against the deadlines that the calendar holds; the autumn is the year's climax, and the climax's shape is now the subject of the work that the assembly's closing has resumed.

The assembly's meaning, in the year's diplomacy, is the meaning that the great room provides every autumn: the demonstration that the world's governments still convene, still speak and still negotiate, whatever the year's conflicts; the demonstration is the institution's value, and the value is the reason the room will fill again. The general assembly closed at the end of September with the record of the year's diplomacy assembled and the work of the autumn's channels resumed; the leaders have returned to their capitals, the negotiators have returned to their files, and the year's business has returned to the rooms where the diplomacy is actually conducted, which is the point of the great room in New York and the work that it makes possible.
