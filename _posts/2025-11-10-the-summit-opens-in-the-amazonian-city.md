The climate summit opened in Belém in the second week of November, and the opening brought the delegations of nearly two hundred countries into the Amazonian city that the gathering's planners had chosen for the symbolism that the location provides. The ceremonies were conducted with the staging that the occasion warrants, the host's speeches, the delegates' statements, the cameras' framing of the river and the forest; the negotiations that the year has prepared began their two-week course, with the texts drafted and the finance question, the gathering's central test, on the table. The opening carried the particular combination of the drama that the location provides and the hard work that the finance question demands, and the combination is the summit's character.

The opening statements were the study in the registers that the year's climate politics have produced. The host country's leaders spoke of the forest and the future, the vulnerable countries' delegates spoke of the emergencies and the funding, and the larger economies' representatives spoke of the progress and the commitments; the statements were the positions' assembly, and the assembly will shape the negotiations that the gathering will conduct. The speeches were the summit's opening act, and the act's substance will be tested by the sessions that follow.

The location's drama is the part of the summit that the coverage has led with, and the drama is the gathering's deliberate choice. The city on the river, the forest on the horizon, the symbolism of the negotiations conducted in the place that the world's largest rainforest makes possible, all of it is the staging that the host has prepared, and the staging is the summit's argument: the gathering's subject is the natural world that the city represents, and the representation is the negotiations' backdrop. The drama is the context, and the context will shape the gathering's conduct.

The finance question is the summit's central test, and the question's opening rounds were conducted with the particular care that the stakes command. The vulnerable countries' delegates pressed the funding that the year's commitments have promised, the developed countries' negotiators offered the language of the progress and the mechanisms, and the gap between the positions was measured in the sessions' exchanges; the test will be conducted through the gathering's two weeks, and the conduct will be the summit's verdict. The finance is the negotiation's subject, and the subject's resolution will be the gathering's product.

The summit's two weeks will be conducted through the sessions that the gathering schedules, and the conduct will be the year's climate record in its final form; the finance will be confronted, the texts will be completed, and the institution's capacity will be tested in the year of the largest economy's absence. The summit opened in the Amazonian city with the delegations assembled and the negotiations begun, and the opening is the gathering's first act; the drama is staged, the positions are set, and the two weeks that follow will determine whether the summit's product matches the drama that its location has promised.
