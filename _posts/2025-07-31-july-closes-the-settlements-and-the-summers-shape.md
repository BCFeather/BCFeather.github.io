The month that carried the European deal, the ceasefire and the Washington talks closed at the end of July, and the closing brought the summer's shape into view. The tariff era's settlements accumulated through the month, the British deal's template extended to the European agreement and the talks that the largest file opened; the war's pause held through its first weeks, with the monitors deploying and the negotiation over the settlement's terms proceeding; and the autumn's calendar, the deadlines, the summits, the sessions, began to cast its shadows over the planning of the governments. July's record is the year's most consequential month so far, and the record's reading is the preparation for the autumn that it sets up.

The trade story of the month was the story of the doctrine's products. The European deal, announced in the month's middle, demonstrated that the tariff era can produce the settlements that its threats are designed to extract, and the demonstration has reshaped the expectations of the markets, the industries and the governments; the Washington talks, opened at the month's end, carried the largest file into the negotiation that the template has made possible. The trade record of July is the record of the settlements' arrival, and the arrivals' consequences will be measured through the autumn's negotiations.

The war's story of the month was the story of the pause. The ceasefire that the leaders' conversation had prepared took effect in the month's middle, the fronts fell quiet along the lines that the negotiators mapped, and the settlement's terms moved into the implementation that the pause requires; the pause has held through its first weeks, and the holding is the war's first sustained hope. The war's record of July is the record of the possibility, and the possibility's fate will be decided by the weeks and the months that the autumn will hold.

The security story of the month was the story of the alliance's renegotiation. The drawdown announcement, delivered in the month's third week, applied the burden-sharing doctrine to the alliance's most concrete commitment, and the application's consequences, the budgets, the plans, the capabilities that the Europeans will now have to produce, will be measured through the year. The alliance's record of July is the record of the change, and the change's shape will be determined by the European response and the security environment that the autumn will bring.

The summer's shape, as July closes, is the shape that the year's second half will live with: the settlements accumulating, the pause holding, the alliance adjusting, and the autumn's calendar scheduled. The preparations that the summer has enabled will be tested in the autumn's sessions and the deadlines, and the testing will be conducted at the pace that the year has established; the month that carried the year's largest settlements closed with the summer's shape in view, and the view is the map that the autumn's work will follow. July's record has been written, and the writing's consequences will be measured in the months that the record has set up, which is the way that the years of change keep their calendars.
