Hi there! I was born and raised in Anhui, a land crossed by the Yangtze and Huai Rivers, where Hui culture and layered local perspectives first shaped how I see the world. I am currently a student at Sichuan University, based in Chengdu.

This blog is my quiet space for sustained observation and reasoned inquiry. I write across two broad fields: I unpack current affairs and social phenomena, tracing the hidden logic beneath surface events; I also follow and analyse advances in technology, with a particular focus on artificial intelligence and the life sciences. I do not chase hot takes or quick, sensational conclusions. Instead, I value slow reading, cross‑referenced facts, and independent thinking — to examine how structures, narratives, emerging technologies and individual lives intersect in our times.

My perspective carries the mark of two places: the rooted, subtle sensibilities from my hometown, and the wider, diverse conversations I encounter during my studies. Writing here is not about claiming final answers. It is an exercise to clarify my own thoughts, to record arguments and observations worth preserving, and to invite calm, respectful dialogue with readers who care about understanding the world more deeply.

All views expressed are my personal reflections, separate from any institutional stance. If you find resonance, disagreement, or questions within these pages, you are welcome to get in touch.
